Real or Robot? Two quick examples

Sample A • Word choice

"In conclusion, the Industrial Revolution was a multifaceted tapestry of innovation that fundamentally reshaped societal paradigms across the globe."

Clue: three AI favorites ("tapestry," "multifaceted," "paradigms") crammed into one sentence.
Sample B • Citation check

"According to Dr. Marcus Vance's 2021 Stanford study on teen sleep cycles, 84% of high schoolers improve test scores after listening to white noise."

Clue: the researcher and the study are made up, and so is the 84%.
